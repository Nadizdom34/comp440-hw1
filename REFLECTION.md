# Reflection — Working with the AI

About one page total, in your own words. Specific beats general: cite actual moments from
your sessions.

## 1. A catch

Describe a specific moment where Claude was wrong, misleading, or overconfident — and how you
caught it. (There is always one. "It never made a mistake" means you weren't checking.)

>

## 2. An override

Describe one judgment call where you redirected or overrode the assistant: what it
proposed or assumed, what you decided instead, and how it turned out.

>

## 3. Next time

What would you delegate to the assistant earlier or more aggressively next time? What would
you keep for yourself? What's your one-sentence rule for when to trust its output?
